# AI Workflow Templates

Three copy‑paste workflow templates you can adapt to your business.

---

## 1. Lead Qualification & Routing

**Goal:** Auto‑qualify inbound leads and route them to the right sales rep or sequence.

**Steps:**

1. **Ingest lead** from form, ads, events (name, email, company, role, UTM, etc.).
2. **Enrich data** via CRM + third‑party (company size, industry, tech stack).
3. **AI scoring**:
   - Input: enriched profile + behavioral signals
   - Output: lead score, fit category (SMB / Mid‑market / Enterprise)
4. **Routing rules**:
   - High score + Enterprise → enterprise queue
   - Medium+ score → SDR queue
   - Low score → nurture sequence
5. **Notify sales** via Slack/email with key context.

**Where AI helps:** Scoring, fit classification, suggested next best action.

---

## 2. Support Ticket Triage

**Goal:** Reduce first response time and manual effort on support tickets.

**Steps:**

1. **Ingest ticket** from helpdesk, email, chat (subject, description, customer, channel).
2. **AI classification**:
   - Category (billing, technical, feature request, etc.)
   - Severity estimate
3. **Prioritization**:
   - Combine severity + customer tier → P1/P2/P3
4. **Routing**:
   - Billing → billing team
   - Technical → engineering support
   - Others → general queue
5. **AI reply draft**:
   - Generate suggested response for agent to review/send.

**Where AI helps:** Understanding free‑text tickets, categorization, prioritization, reply drafting.

---

## 3. Data Enrichment & Account Scoring

**Goal:** Keep customer records enriched and scored for sales/CS playbooks.

**Steps:**

1. **Fetch records** from CRM (e.g., active accounts not enriched in 30 days).
2. **Gather signals**:
   - Product usage metrics
   - Billing/invoice data
   - Third‑party firmographics
3. **Build unified profile** from all sources.
4. **AI scoring**:
   - Health score
   - Upsell propensity
   - Churn risk
5. **Update CRM** with new fields; trigger playbooks (e.g., at‑risk accounts).

**Where AI helps:** Inferring scores and risks from complex, multi‑source data.
